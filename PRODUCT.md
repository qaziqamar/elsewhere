# PRODUCT — Scrolless

## Users
Busy 18-35, knowledge workers & students, reading fast, often late-night, guilt about doomscrolling.

## Mode
Operate + Reflect. Dashboard helps user input weekly screen time per app, see breakdown, feel impact, export PNG proof.

## Single Job
Answer in 10s: "How much of my life did I scroll away this week?" and make it shareable.

## Brand Voice
Provocative but calm, clinical, no hype. Punchy hooks: "Reclaim your hours from the digital abyss."

## Anti-References
No purple gradients as default, no glassmorphism everywhere, no Inter default, no centered-hero-over-mesh, no 3-equal-cards, no em-dashes, no nested cards.

## Success
User inputs times → charts update live → impact metric hits emotionally → PNG export succeeds 2x crisp on dark bg.
